# Project guidance

## Purpose
Build an Indian-market trading assistant for NSE/BSE, NIFTY, BANK NIFTY, equities, futures and options.

## Principles
- Prefer analysis and paper trading over live execution.
- Never imply a trade is guaranteed to profit.
- Always state assumptions, timeframe and data freshness.
- Include entry, invalidation/stop, targets and risk/reward when producing a trade setup.
- Respect Indian market hours, expiries, lot sizes and instrument conventions.
- Keep broker credentials and secrets out of source control.
