# Indian Market Checklist

Before producing a trade plan, check:

- Exchange: NSE or BSE
- Instrument and symbol
- Cash/F&O segment
- Timeframe
- Current price and data timestamp
- Trend and market structure
- Key support/resistance
- Volume/open interest where available
- Option expiry and contract/lot-size details
- Relevant corporate/news events
- Entry trigger
- Stop/invalidation
- Target(s)
- Position size and maximum rupee risk
- Risk/reward
- Whether the setup is paper-trading or live

Do not fabricate unavailable market data.
